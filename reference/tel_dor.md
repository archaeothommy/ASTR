# LIA points of silver hoard from Tell Dor (Israel)

Lead isotope ratios of a Phoenician silver hoard from Tel Dor. Reference
data set of isotope ratios using
[`as_ASTR()`](https://archaeothommy.github.io/ASTR/reference/ASTR.md)

## Usage

``` r
tel_dor
```

## Format

ASTR object with 3 Pb isotope variables

- 206Pb/204Pb:

  Lead isotope ratio of 206Pb/204Pb.

- 207Pb/204Pb:

  Lead isotope ratio of 207Pb/204Pb.

- 208Pb/204Pb:

  Lead isotope ratio of 208Pb/204Pb.

## Source

Eshel, T., Erel, Y., Yahalom-Mack, N., Tirosh, O., and Gilboa, A.
(2019). Lead isotopes in silver reveal earliest Phoenician quest for
metals in the west Mediterranean. Proceedings of the National Academy of
Sciences 116(13), 6007–6012. <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1817951116>
